Research & publications

Attention. Participation.
Ecological relationships.

My research connects ecological art education, embodied learning, and place-based practice. I am interested in how learning with plants and local environments can open up different ways of relating to the more-than-human world.

Master’s research · CAFA · 2026

Plants as a medium
for ecological art education.

My thesis develops a teaching model connecting pedagogical philosophy, learning relationships, curriculum content, and practice. It explores plant signals, growth processes, and survival strategies through perception, participation, and situated making.
